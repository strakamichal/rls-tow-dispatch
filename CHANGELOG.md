# Changelog

What changed for players in each version of Tow Dispatch.

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

The first release: run a tow business in your RLS career.

### Tow jobs
- Drivers whose cars broke down call dispatch. Load the car on your tow truck or car trailer
  and take it to the owner's home or to a dealership for service.
- Four breakdowns, each one you can see and feel: a nearly empty tank, a flat tyre, an
  overheating engine with no coolant left and a failing engine that misfires and stalls.
  Every car still drives a few metres, enough to get it onto a flatbed.
- Some cars also look a bit shabby. That is only paint; it does not change the job.

### The Tow Dispatch app
- A new app in the RLS phone, styled like a tow company's dispatch screen.
- A job board on every map with 10–14 calls of every tier, from cheap runabouts to exotics
  and heavy trucks. New calls come in over time and each one expires on its own clock.
- Every call shows the payout, the fault, the car and its weight, how far it is from you, how
  long the tow is and where it goes, before you accept it.
- Sort calls by payout or distance and filter them by tier and distance.
- Calls above your Recovery level are shown as locked, with the level that unlocks them.
- **Navigate** sets the route to the car and then to the destination.
- A short guide behind the **?** button.

### Pay and progression
- The payout is agreed up front. Short and long tows pay about the same per minute, so long
  routes are worth taking. A tow of about 6 km pays a little more per minute than RLS repo work
  (repo pays more for very short drives, towing for long ones). Exotics and heavy trucks pay the
  most, about as much as a Job Broker delivery of one.
- Jobs give XP to the RLS Recovery skill, shared with repo and Off-Road Recovery. Tiers unlock
  at the same levels as RLS repo, and a job that levels you up says so.
- Towing pays, driving does not: driving the customer's car a short way onto your truck is
  fine, but driving it to the destination yourself cuts the pay and gives no XP.
- Damage you cause on the way comes off the payout, at most half of it.
- Cancelling is free until you move the car; after that it costs a fine.
- Start with a pickup and a car trailer rented at the Job Broker depot; once the rig is your
  own, every tow pays 10 % more (5 % with your own truck and a rented trailer).

### Part of your career
- Payouts show up in Wallet as recovery income, and finished jobs in the logbook.
- The board and an active job are saved with your career, including a car loaded on a
  flatbed. A job waits on its map while you travel.
- Uses RLS parking reservations, so Tow Dispatch and RLS jobs never pick the same spot.
- Works alongside the original RLS repo jobs and RLS Carjacking & Theft.
- Warns in the app when the installed RLS is not 2.8.x.
